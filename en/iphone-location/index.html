FAKEGPS · IPHONE LOCATION TOOL

iPhone Virtual Location Tool

FakeGPS is a free, open-source utility for setting a temporary virtual GPS location on an iPhone. Connect over USB, choose a place, and apply it instantly.

Free to useOpen sourceWindows / macOSGPX playback

What FakeGPS does

Choose a location by clicking the map, searching for a place, or entering latitude and longitude. FakeGPS supports AMap and global map sources, with automatic handling for GCJ-02 and WGS-84 coordinates.

Pick on the map

Click a destination and apply the temporary location to your connected iPhone.

Search places

Find cities, landmarks, and addresses, or enter coordinates directly.

Play GPX routes

Import a GPX file and replay its route using the timestamps and speed data.

How to set a virtual iPhone location

  1. Install FakeGPS and connect your iPhone with a data-capable USB cable
  2. Trust the computer on your iPhone and complete the setup prompts
  3. Choose a location on the map and click Set Location

FakeGPS supports iOS 16 and later. iOS 17 and later may ask for permission to start the tunneling service.

Free, open source, and privacy-conscious

FakeGPS does not require a jailbreak or an app installed on the iPhone. The project source is available on GitHub, and location data is not uploaded to a third-party tracking service. Use the software responsibly and follow local laws.

Frequently asked questions

Does FakeGPS require a jailbreak?

No. FakeGPS communicates with the iPhone from your computer over USB.

Does it work on Windows and macOS?

Yes. Download the Windows installer or open the macOS DMG and drag FakeGPS into Applications.

Can it play GPX files?

Yes. Playback follows the route timing and speed data stored in the GPX file.
